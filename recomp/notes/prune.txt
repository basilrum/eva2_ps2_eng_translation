2026-09-04: `j` is now read two ways instead of one.

A `j` whose source lies inside the function above the candidate is an
intra-function branch, and the candidate is a continuation -- that was the only
reading before, and it is still right.

A `j` whose source lies OUTSIDE that range is a TAIL CALL, and the candidate is
its callee: a real function that simply never appears as a `jal` target. Adding
that reading moved 701 functions from "unreferenced" to "reached".

The two readings are complements of the same test, so nothing had to be
re-tuned. This only became visible after func_scan learned to end functions at
tail calls; before that the callees mostly were not in the list at all.
